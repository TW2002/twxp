     Strips planets of resources and places them on starting planet.  
      
     Usage: strip [planet# | all]  {options}
       
            Options:
                 {f}   - Strip fuel ore
                 {o}   - Strip organics
                 {e}   - Strip equipment
                {fc}   - Strip fuel ore colonists
                {oc}   - Strip organic colonists
                {ec}   - Strip equipment colonists
               {fig}   - Strip fighters
                {sh}   - Strip shields
               {all}   - Strip everything
            {silent}   - Silence comms
              {deaf}   - Hide display while moving products
