     - kazi [planet] {shields} {defender} {zdy}
         Automates planet invasion. 
                                
         [planet]               
            - Planet number to attack   
         [shields]                      
            - Will kill planetary shields. Stops when below 50. 
         [defender]                                        
            - Will land defensively to take out military reaction.
         [zdy]                         
            - Option to blow planet as soon as you land.
