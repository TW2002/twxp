                     
   
                <<<< plimper >>>>
   
     Keeps personal limpets loaded in the current sector.
        
     Usage:  plimper {limit} {saveme} {credits:xxxxxxx}
        
         {limit} - minimum limpets in sector (default: 250).
        {saveme} - run saveme while keeping limps current.
       {credits} - minimum credits to furb (default: 5000000)
        
        Run from Citadel, blue alignment, with T-warp and Stardock known.
        Refurbs limpets at Stardock and reloads the sector after limp hits.
