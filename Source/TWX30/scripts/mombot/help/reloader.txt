     reloader [on/off] [fig minimum] {ig} {topoff} {fig}
       - Sector Reloader Mode
         Sits above planet and lands/reloads fighters when hit.
       
         Options: 
                [on/off]   Turns Reloader On or Off
           [fig minimum]   Number of ship fighters to lose before 
                           landing and refilling
                    [ig]   Reset IG if photoned 
                [topoff]   Uses fighters in sector first 
                [noland]   Do not land - should be running citfill
