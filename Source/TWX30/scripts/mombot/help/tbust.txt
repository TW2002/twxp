                     
   
                 <<<< tbust >>>>
   
     Traitors Planet Buster modified for M()M Bot use.
            
       Usage: tbust [experience] {safe} {2fer} {max} {override}
                    {delay} {bank} {red}
            
     Options:
        [experience]  Desired experience.
        {safe}        Create and destroy one at a time.
        {2fer}        Create and destroy two at a time.
        {max}         Create and destroy the max amount.
        {override}    Override turns low limit.
        {delay}       Random delay for each bust.
        {bank}        Corpie will pass credits through bank.
        {red}         Attempt negative alignment.
