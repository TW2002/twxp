         Ram gridder for mombot based on LoneStars chains algorithm
         Uses chain selection first, then falls back to single sectors.
         Best when there are clusters of adjacent unfigged sectors.
         REFRESH FIG LIST!
         
         tram [stop_turns] [stop_fighters] {saveme} {quiet} {burst:n}
            
      Options:
         
        [stop_turns]     stop when you get to these turns 
        [stop_fighters]  stop when you get to these fighters
        {saveme}  when gridder is stuck it will call saveme to be safe
        {quiet}   suppress running Total Gridded / Efficiency messages
        {burst:n} number of macros to burst at once, default 4
