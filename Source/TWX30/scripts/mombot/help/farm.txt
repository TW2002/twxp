                     
   
                  <<<< farm >>>>
   
     Visits sectors in list and farms the planets there.
            
       Usage:  farm set {sector1} {sector2} {...}
       Usage:  farm list
       Usage:  farm clear
       Usage:  farm balance
       Usage:  farm fill {planets}/{all} {options}
       Usage:  farm 
            
     Examples:
            
       >farm set 1224 1925 3176     
       >farm     
       >farm fill 12 13 14     
       >farm fill all     
            
     Modes:
            
        set - Adds sectors in the order entered into the farm set.
        list - Lists all sectors in the farm set.
        clear - Removes all sectors from the farm set.
        balance - Attempts to balance colos on farm planets.
           
        Running farm with no options attempts to farm all products.
        If you specify one or more options, only those will be farmed.
        Planet numbers or all specify planets to be filled.
            
            Product Options:
                 {f}   - Farm fuel ore
                 {o}   - Farm organics
                 {e}   - Farm equipment
                {fc}   - Farm fuel ore colonists
                {oc}   - Farm organic colonists
                {ec}   - Farm equipment colonists
               {fig}   - Farm fighters
                {sh}   - Farm shields
