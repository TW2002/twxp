     bwarp {sector:#} {"trader_name"} {p}
           
             planet transports to sector
            
         Options: 
                {sector:#} - sector to bwarp to 
           {"trader_name"} - trader to bwarp to
                       {p} - port after bwarping in 
              
         Examples:
                    >b 233 - normal bwarp
                  >b 233 p - bwarp to sector, and port 
              >b planet 12 - bwarp to last known 
                             location of planet 12 
                   >b mind - bwarp to a corp member with mind
                             in their name
          >b "mind dagger" - bwarp to corp member
