                     
   
                  <<<< mxex >>>>
   
     Move/xport/attack using a moth ship.
        
     Usage:  mxex [target sector] [moth ship] {tow ship}
        
        [target sector] - adjacent sector to attack.
        [moth ship] - ship to xport from/through.
        {tow ship} - optional ship to tow.
        Run from Command or Citadel.
