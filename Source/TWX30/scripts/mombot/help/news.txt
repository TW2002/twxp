     Reads and reports daily game news.
      
     news [category] {r|yest}
      
     Categories:
             {rep}    Overall reporting of events in the Log
           {foton}    Lists fotons fired
             {tow}    Who was towed
           {ports}    Port activity
         {planets}    Who popped planet(s) and how many
            {corp}    Corporate news
             {fed}    Commish awards and bounties
            {pods}    Itemized list of who podded
       {overloads}    Sectors with overloaded planets
        {announce}    Announcements made
      
     Refresh:
               {r}    Refreshes using current game date
            {yest}    Refreshes previous day game date data
