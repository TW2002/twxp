                     
   
                  <<<< wrob >>>>
   
     Travels universe robbing ports.
            
       Usage: wrob [minimum rob amount] {upgraded} {skipcim} {clear_empty}
            
     Options:
        [minimum rob amount]  Amount that must be on port before robbing.
        {upgraded}            Only visit upgraded ports.
        {skipcim}             Skip CIM port report before running.
        {clear_empty}         Delete the empty port file.
