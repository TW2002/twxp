                     
   
                  <<<< mex >>>>
   
     Move/attack/export using a safe ship.
        
     Usage:  mex [target sector] [safe ship] {tow ship}
        
        [target sector] - adjacent sector to attack.
        [safe ship] - ship to xport into after attack.
        {tow ship} - optional ship to tow.
        Run from Command or Citadel.
