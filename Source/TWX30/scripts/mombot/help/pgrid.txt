     pgrid - planet grid into sector 
        
       Usage: pgrid <sector> [wave count] [options]
        
            Options: 
        
              {scan} - Do a density scan of the sector before gridding
            {unsafe} - Send fast macro, dont wait for planet
               {f:x} - Drop x fighters after gridding into sector
               {x:y} - xport to ship y after gridding
               {d:x} - Maximum density to grid into
                 {r} - Move and immediately retreat from sector
             {nosur} - Dont surrender on retreat
