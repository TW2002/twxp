     GPM (GoPop Moo) version 5.0 for TWX Proxy 3.0
     Designed by Rider for Mayhem Corp in ICE 2019
     Authors: Rider, Shadow and LoneStar    
         
     Walks the universe, creates (pops) planets and sells off product for cash.  
         
     Options: 
              {corp} - Pop corporate planets instead of personal
           {skipore} - Doesnt keep popping planets until ore is gone
           {skiporg} - Doesnt keep popping planets until organics are gone
           {skipequ} - Doesnt keep popping planets until equipment is gone
             {upequ} - Upgrade equipment port if MCIC is good
             {probe} - Buy and use ether probes to find ports while cashing
          {noaliens} - Avoid sectors with aliens in them
           {buyfigs} - Buy fighters at dock if we have extra cash after furbing
        {buyshields} - Buy shields at dock if we have extra cash after furbing
         {twarponly} - Only use twarp to move, dont move to adjacent sectors
            {window} - Open status window for dashboard summary
