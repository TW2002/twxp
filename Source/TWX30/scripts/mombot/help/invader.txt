       invader is a wrapper for the following commands:
       pe, ped, pel, pelk, pex, pxe, pxed, pxedx, pxel, pxelk, pxex
          
       usage:
          
       pe [Sector] - Launch photon into adjacent sector and immediately enter 
       ped [Sector] - Launch photon, enter and launch genesis torpedo 
       pel [Sector] [Planet#] - Photon, enter and land on planet 
       pelk [Sector] [Planet#] - Photon, enter, land and send one wave of fighters 
       pex [Sector] [Ship#] - Photon, enter and export to another ship 
       pxe [Sector] [Ship#] - Photon, export to another ship and enter 
       pxed [Sector] [Ship#] - Photon, export, enter and launch genesis torpedo 
       pxel [Sector] [Ship#] [Planet#] - Photon, export, enter and land on planet 
       pxelk [Sector] [Ship#] [Planet#] - Photon, export, enter, land and send wave 
       pxex [Sector] [Ship#] - Photon, export, enter and export back 
          
       Examples:
          
       >pe 24902 
       >pel 24902 15
       >pxel 24902 3 15
