                     
   
                <<<< runaway >>>>
   
     Pwarps to random safe flee sectors after fighter hits.
        
     Usage:  runaway [on | off] {first sector} {evac}
        
        [on] - enable runaway mode.
        [off] - disable runaway mode.
        {first sector} - optional first flee sector to use.
        {evac} - move all planets to the flee sector instead of just pwarping.
        Run from Citadel. Builds a flee-sector list and reacts to fig hits.
