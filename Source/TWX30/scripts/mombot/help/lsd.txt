     LoneStar Dock Shopper order runner.
        
     Usage:  lsd [encoded order]
        
        [encoded order] - dock shopping order to execute.
        Runs a Stardock shopping order for hardware, holds, figs,
        shields, ship purchase, towing, and upgrades.
        Usually launched by the dock shopper menu.
        Use m for max and 0/blank for no purchase in order fields.
