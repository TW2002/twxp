     twarp {sector:#} {"trader_name"} {p} 
           
             transwarps to sector as quickly 
             and safely as possible.   
           
         Options: 
                {sector:#} - sector to twarp to 
           {"trader_name"} - trader to twarp to
                       {p} - attempt to port after twarp
              
         Examples:
                 >t 233    - normal twarp
                 >t 233 12 - twarp, then land on planet 12
                 >t 233 p  - twarp, then port
              >t planet 12 - twarp to last known 
                             location of planet 12 and land
                   >t mind - twarp to a corp member with mind
                             in their name
          >t "mind dagger" - twarp to corp member
