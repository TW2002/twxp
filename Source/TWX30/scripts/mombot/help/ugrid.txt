                     
   
                 <<<< ugrid >>>>
   
     Ultimate gridder. Visits all targeted sectors.
            
       Usage: ugrid [targeting] {figs} {armids} {limpets} {safety}
                    {planets} {warp} {refurb} {scrub} {avoid}
                    {aggressive} {passive} {clear}
            
     Options:
        [targeting]  Filename to pull targets from or auto.
        [figs]       Fighters to drop. Default: 1.
        [armids]     Armid mines to drop. Default: 3.
        [limps]      Limpet mines to drop. Default: 3.
        [safety]     ultra, safe, or none. Default: ultra.
        [planets]    all, shielded, or none. Default: all.
        [warp]       twarp or bwarp. Default: twarp.
        {norefurb}   Turns off auto-refurbing mines at Stardock.
        {scrub}      Scrub at dock when catching a limpet.
        {avoid}      Avoid sectors with enemy limpets.
        {aggressive} Do not avoid big fighter groups.
        {passive}    Avoid hitting player fighters or mines.
        {clear}      Clears internal avoided-sector list.
