                     
   
                <<<< movefig >>>>
   
     Moves fighters between planet(s) and sector.
        
     Usage:  movefig [p | s] [amount | all] {all}
        
        [p] - move sector fighters to the planet.
        [s] - move planet fighters to the sector.
        [amount | all] - fighter amount to move.
        {all} with s - pull fighters from all planets in sector.
        Run from Planet or Citadel.
