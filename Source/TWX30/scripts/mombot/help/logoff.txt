     logoff {minutes} {cloak}
       - logs off now and optionally relogs after a timer
     logout {minutes} {cloak}
       - alias for logoff
     Examples:
       >logoff        - log off until manually restarted
       >logoff 10     - log off and relog in 10 minutes
       >logout cloak  - cloak and log off until manually restarted
       >logoff 30 cloak - cloak out and relog in 30 minutes
