                     
   
                <<<< upgrade >>>>
   
     Upgrades planets using products and colonists in sector.
     Products and Colos must on a port or planet in sector
      
        Usage:   
      
        >upgrade   
        >upgrade 4 6 10
        >upgrade ignore 3 7 11
      
        Upgrades all planets in sector by default. 
        {planets} will only upgrade the specified planets
        {ignore [planets]} will skip the specified planets
