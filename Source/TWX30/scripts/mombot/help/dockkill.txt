                     
   
                <<<< dockkill >>>>
   
     Scans for targets and autokills in sector.
              
       Options: 
           {off} - Turns off script 
           {pod} - Only shoots pods
          {meat} - meatgrinder mode
           {cap} - capture instead of kill
            {dt} - doubletap mode
            {sg} - shotgun mode
      {defender} - pops a planet before attacking
