     Searches database sectors by port, MCIC, figs, and warp count.
        
     Usage:  list {port type} {mcic} {pair} {figged} {warp filter}
        
     Arguments:
        {port type}   sbb/bbs/xxx/xbb/xxb/etc. B buys, S sells, X is any.
             {mcic}   positive or negative MCIC threshold.
             {pair}   require a matching paired adjacent port.
           {figged}   include figged sectors instead of unfigged sectors.
      {warp filter}   deadend, 2way, 3way, 4way, 5way, 6way, or 7way.
