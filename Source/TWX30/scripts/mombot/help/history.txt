      history {limit:#} {filter:x}
        
          Displays the most recent self commands
          this bot has given.
              
           {limit:#} - display the last # of commands
          {filter:x} - only show commands matching this
                           
          Example:                      
              >history 10                      
              >history pdrop                   
              >history 3 pdrop
