     - foton [on|off|sec] {a|d|p|s|d|t} {towship} {sector} 
                          {return} {den40}
       Multiple use photon script.  
       
       Options: 
         {a}djacent - photons adjacent sector when
                      fig/limp/armid hit
         {d}ensity  - constant density scan, photons
                      on density change
         {p}lanet   - standard planet warp photon script
         {s}urround - attempts to foton retreat sector
         d{o}ck     - sits on dock and attempts to foton
                      on adjacent fig hit
         {t}ow      - twarp tow a photon in a second ship
            
         {towship}  - Ship in sector with photon you will tow
         {sector}   - Apply the mode from/to that sector
         {return}   - Returns Planet Home after Pwarp
          {den40}   - Only shoots on 40 to 499 Density Change
           {holo}   - does holo command after firing
        {dockexp}   - Will pop planet to get to 1k
                      for dock photon
           {self}   - Will pwarp out, photon your current 
                      sector, and pwarp back in. 
           {cont}   - Will continue shooting if in density mode.
      {delwalk:n}   - Delay walk will delay the shot for this many MS.
                      Then add another 100ms to subsequent hit.
